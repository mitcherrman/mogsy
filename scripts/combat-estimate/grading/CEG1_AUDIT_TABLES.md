## 13. Why a fixed point tolerance cannot scale [verified bound + measured]

Rounding the armor multiplier m to 2 dp moves the answer by at most 0.005 / m of itself — a relative bound, independent of magnitude. On the certified after-armor rounds m spans 0.430–0.806, so a correct-method player's 2-dp rounding can cost up to 1.2%: 11.6 points on a hypothetical 1 000-damage answer, outside any ±3 or ±5 absolute rule. The relative part of the hybrid keeps that player correct at large magnitudes; the absolute floor keeps a small answer from demanding exactness.
